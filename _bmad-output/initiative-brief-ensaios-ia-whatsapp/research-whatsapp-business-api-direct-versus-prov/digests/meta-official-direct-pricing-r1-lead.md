# Digest — Meta direct integration, pricing, and current policy — round 1

Accessed 2026-10-27. Sources are Meta-owned business pages and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform collection on Postman.

- **Claim:** Meta explicitly offers two integration routes: direct API access for a business with developers who can call the APIs and set up webhooks, or an approved Business Solution Provider for a business that prefers help with onboarding/support or an integrated inbox. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/ — WhatsApp Business, publication date not shown, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high; **class:** implementation/selection.
- **Claim:** There is no extra fee to access the WhatsApp Business Platform directly; Meta charges its message fees. A partner may add fees for its extra services or technology. Meta offers a Direct Support portal for businesses using its API directly. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/ — WhatsApp Business, publication date not shown, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high; **class:** pricing/support.
- **Claim:** Effective 2026-10-01, the first 1,000 service messages per business phone number each month are free; Meta charges its published per-message rates beginning with message 1,001. The public pricing page still says service messages are free without reflecting this October change, so Meta's FAQ and pricing page conflict on this point. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/ and https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/ — WhatsApp Business, publication date not shown, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high that FAQ states the effective rule and the pages conflict; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Meta prices delivered messages by recipient market and message category (marketing, utility, authentication, service); it says a user-initiated conversation opens a 24-hour service window and describes service messages as including replies from a conversational AI bot. The current Brazil rate card is dynamically selected and its exact rate was not exposed in the public page content retrieved for this research. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/ — WhatsApp Business, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high on model, medium on exact fee due to dynamic rates; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Meta's Developer Hub offers test phone numbers, code samples, webhooks, and a sandbox at no cost. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/developers/developer-hub/ — WhatsApp Business, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high; **class:** implementation/cost.
- **Claim:** Meta's official Cloud API collection supports sending text, media, and templates through the `/{Phone-Number-ID}/messages` endpoint; it requires a registered phone-number ID and a token with `whatsapp_business_messaging`, and delivery status can be tracked through webhooks. **Source:** https://www.postman.com/meta/whatsapp-business-platform/folder/o48mro7/messages — Meta WhatsApp Business Platform collection on Postman, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high; **class:** implementation.
- **Claim:** Meta's Business Messaging Policy, last updated 2026-09-23, permits automated replies within the 24-hour window but requires a clear way to escalate to a human or direct support channel. It requires opt-in for subsequent messages and approved templates to initiate conversations outside the window. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ — WhatsApp Business, updated 2026-09-23; **confidence:** high; **class:** implementation/operations.
- **Claim:** Meta's Business Platform FAQ describes a separate token-based charge for Meta Business Agent. This research's project would call its own AI image service through its own backend, not Meta Business Agent; no official source found here assigning the separate Business Agent charge to that pattern. **Source:** https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/faq/ — WhatsApp Business, publication date not shown, accessed 2026-10-27; **confidence:** high for the distinction, unverified for any other AI-provider pricing classification; **class:** pricing/open question.

**Leads:** Verify the current Brazil service-message rate against Meta's rate-card file or a live Business Manager billing view if a precise budget is required. Confirm the right product-level classification for the project's AI image-generation bot only if a provider quotes a separate AI-related charge.

**Looked for but not established:** The exact current Brazil price for service messages was not rendered in Meta's dynamic public calculator. The policy and FAQ pages do not identify a separate fee for an ordinary business using its own AI workflow; they identify a separate rate for Meta Business Agent.
