# Digest — Twilio Brazil fit, price, support, and portability — round 1

Researcher digest; official Twilio sources only. Accessed 2026-10-27.

- **Claim:** Twilio offers WhatsApp through its Programmable Messaging API, with a Node SDK and official Node/Express examples for sending/receiving messages, webhooks, and media. **Sources:** https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/tutorial/send-and-receive-media-messages-whatsapp-nodejs ; https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/api . **Publisher/date:** Twilio, n.d. **Confidence:** high; **class:** implementation.
- **Claim:** Twilio's per-message fee is USD 0.005 inbound or outbound, on top of Meta's destination/category fee. Its WhatsApp pricing CSV lists Brazil at USD 0.0625 marketing and USD 0.0068 utility, authentication, and service. It also lists USD 0.001 for messages that end `Failed`. **Sources:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/whatsapp/pricing ; https://www.twilio.com/content/dam/twilio-com/pricing-data/en/WhatsAppPricing-pricing-details.csv . **Publisher/date:** Twilio; pricing page says current as of 2026-09, CSV n.d. **Confidence:** high for vendor-listed rates, medium for service-window application; **class:** pricing.
- **Caveat:** Twilio's pricing page and help/usage documents conflict about whether Meta bills service and utility messages inside the 24-hour window. Twilio's own USD 0.005 message fee still applies. The pilot's exact service-window total needs confirmation before cost modeling. **Sources:** the pricing page above; https://help.twilio.com/articles/360037672734-How-Much-Does-it-Cost-to-Send-and-Receive-WhatsApp-Messages-with-Twilio- ; https://www.twilio.com/docs/usage/api/usage-record . **Publisher/date:** Twilio, pricing page says current as of 2026-09; other pages n.d. **Confidence:** high that vendor documents conflict; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Twilio's free Developer support has no response-time guarantee and estimates three business days. Paid Production support is the greater of USD 250/month or 4% of monthly spend; Business support is the greater of USD 1,500/month or 6% of spend. **Source:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/support-plans — Twilio, n.d., accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** high; **class:** support/pricing.
- **Claim:** Twilio supports migrations into its service from another WhatsApp Platform provider; sender details and templates can migrate, but templates are duplicated/re-reviewed and some may be rejected. The docs list prerequisites including a verified Meta portfolio, approved source WABA, valid payment method, and two-factor authentication disabled. **Source:** https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/migrate-numbers-and-senders — Twilio, n.d., accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** high; **class:** portability.
- **Claim:** Twilio links one WABA to an account/subaccount, and changing the sender's Twilio account requires support; outbound portability of a sender/WABA/templates intact was not found in Twilio-owned docs. **Sources:** https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/key-concepts ; https://www.twilio.com/docs/whatsapp/migrate-numbers-and-senders . **Publisher/date:** Twilio, n.d., accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** high for account constraint; medium for the documentation gap; **class:** portability.
- **Unverified:** No Twilio-owned page found explicitly confirming eligibility for a Brazil-incorporated business and +55 sender. **Class:** coverage/evidence gap.

**Leads:** Confirm service-window charges with Twilio's account calculator/support if Twilio remains shortlisted; compare its paid support minimums against direct Meta support and 360dialog/Zenvia support.
