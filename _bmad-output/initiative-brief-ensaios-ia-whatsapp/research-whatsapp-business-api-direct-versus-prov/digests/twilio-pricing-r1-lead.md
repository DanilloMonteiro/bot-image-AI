# Digest — Twilio pricing and fee model — round 1

Accessed 2026-10-27. Claims below are from vendor-owned pages and still need dimension verification against Meta's current rate card.

- **Claim:** Twilio's WhatsApp Programmable Messaging price is USD 0.005 per inbound or outbound message, in addition to Meta's applicable template-message fees passed through to the customer. **Source:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/whatsapp/pricing — Twilio, page says pricing current as of 2026-09, accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** high for the listed Twilio fee; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Twilio says failed message processing can add USD 0.001 for messages that end in `Failed`; the page says prices may change and excludes add-on features. **Source:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/whatsapp/pricing — Twilio, page says pricing current as of 2026-09, accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** high for the listed fee; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Twilio's help article distinguishes fees by API: Programmable Messaging is per message; Conversations may add a monthly-active-user fee; Flex uses a seat/hour pricing model. **Source:** https://help.twilio.com/articles/360037672734-How-Much-Does-it-Cost-to-Send-and-Receive-WhatsApp-Messages-with-Twilio- — Twilio Help Center, updated date not shown in search result (n.d.), accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** medium pending direct page check; **class:** pricing.
- **Claim:** Twilio says its sandbox/free trial can be used to start building; live use remains usage-priced. **Source:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/whatsapp/pricing — Twilio, page says pricing current as of 2026-09, accessed 2026-10-27. **Confidence:** medium (vendor statement); **class:** implementation/support.

**Leads:** Verify the Meta Brazil rate card separately, identify whether the project's likely customer-service traffic is mostly inside the 24-hour reply window, and compare Twilio's added per-message layer with the direct Meta route.

**Looked for but not established:** This search batch did not establish Twilio's Brazil business-onboarding steps, whether a third-party fee is charged for media/webhooks beyond per-message pricing, or a non-marketing support SLA.
